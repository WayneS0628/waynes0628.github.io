title: Signal was one verb
summary: Why this site's big idea changed from "signal over noise" to "I build AI to get things done."
date: 2026-10-23T20:00:00Z
---

When I rebuilt this site, I built it around one idea: [signal over noise](/writing/signal-over-noise/). It was true. It was also too narrow, and a little too science-y. It made me sound like someone who only stares at data until it gives something up.

Finding the signal is one thing I do. It isn't the whole job. When I looked at what I've actually built, most of it wasn't about finding anything. It was about getting something done for someone.

So the big idea is simpler now: **I build AI to get things done.** Signal over noise didn't go anywhere. It became one of five verbs:

- **Find.** Pull insight out of messy data: [contract triage for Accenture](/work/cuad-contract-triage/), [what 114,000 songs sound like](/work/spotify-tracks-explorer/), [decision tree vs neural network](/work/titanic-dtvsnn/).
- **Answer.** Inform people with grounded answers: [a RAG chatbot on the Claude API](/work/claude-rag-chatbot/).
- **Simplify.** Make life easier: [CleanTube](/work/cleantube/).
- **Anticipate.** Act before you have to ask. This one is next: an [autonomous data analyst agent](/work/autonomous-data-analyst/) I'm building in my November and December sprint. It's marked `[next]` until it's real.
- **Delight.** Do cool things: this site, with a lofi loop generated live in your browser and a [latent-space map](/#map) of my projects.

"Done" is the word that ties it together. Finished projects are marked `[done]`. The loading screen trains until the target is done. The homepage dictionary defines it as the only status that counts.

Hire me if you want to get things done.
