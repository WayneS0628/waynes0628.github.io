Contracts are long, and the clauses that matter (liability caps, termination rights, exclusivity) are buried in them. This project builds a pipeline that finds those clauses in a contract and triages them by risk, so a reviewer can start with what actually needs attention.

It's a team project from the Break Through Tech AI Studio, built for Accenture on the public [CUAD dataset](https://www.atticusprojectai.org/cuad).
